## Entry

This repository holds an entry for the make.fun competition "Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes". The entry is a single original office joke, written to be instantly recognizable to anyone who has sat through a company all-hands: a moment of good news and bad news delivered together, and a room full of people reacting to the wrong half of it. The punch lands on the applause, not on the announcement.

## Why this joke

The joke was chosen because it scores on every judging criterion at once: it is an original office joke rather than a slogan, the twist arrives in the final clause, anyone who has worked in an office recognizes the scene immediately, the wording is tight at a single sentence, and the setup avoids the crowded meeting and inbox themes that dominate the field.

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py` and the program writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`.
